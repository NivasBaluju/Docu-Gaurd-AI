SERVICES LEVEL AGREEMENT

1. Definitions
1.1 "Services" refers to cloud computing services described in Order Forms.
1.2 "Uptime Target" refers to 99.95% monthly uptime.

2. Performance Standards
Vendor shall deliver Services with 99.95% availability during each calendar month.
If Vendor fails to meet Uptime Target, Customer shall receive a 10% service credit.

3. Payment Terms
Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt via electronic funds transfer.

4. Termination
Either party may terminate upon thirty (30) days written notice for uncured material breach.

5. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delaware without regard to conflict of laws principles.
