CONFIDENTIALITY AND MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Section 1. Confidential Information.
"Confidential Information" means all non-public proprietary data disclosed by Discloser to Recipient.

Section 2. Standard of Care.
Recipient agrees to use at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

Section 3. Term of Protection.
The obligations of confidentiality shall survive for five (5) years from the date of disclosure.
